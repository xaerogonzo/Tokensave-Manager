### Added
- (doctor) **Doctor reports an unreadable or un-ignored agent handoff ledger.** Silent when a project has no ledger, which is nearly all of them. An unopenable ledger says "could not determine" and never reads as empty. Lines the ledger had to drop are now counted here, the only place a person learns that a listing is shorter than the file. A ledger in the project-folder fallback is checked against git: not ignored is named as the risk, and git not answering is its own statement. Warn-only, not counted as a violation. (`src/helpers/doctor_rules.py`, `src/controllers/doctor_ctrl.py`, `tests/test_doctor_rules.py`)
