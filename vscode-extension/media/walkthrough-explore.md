## What the extension adds

**Projects** — one root per workspace folder, with the Manager's operations
under it. Results appear beside each row.

**Savings & Spend** — savings from `gain`, spend from `cost`. These are
different numbers and the panel keeps them apart.

**Test Explorer** — your `tests/` tree at test granularity. Two Run profiles:
everything, or the `not tk` gate that CI enforces. There is no Debug profile;
that is the Python extension's job and it does it properly.

**Problems** — findings from `checks`, `doctor` and `scout`, on the line each
refers to.

**Command palette** — everything above, plus the Manager's own dialogs under
**TokenSave Manager**.
